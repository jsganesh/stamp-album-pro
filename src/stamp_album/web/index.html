    Welcome to StampAlbum Pro

    1 Add stamps at their catalogue size
    Click a shape in the palette, or drag it onto the page. Type the stamp's size from the catalogue into W and H: the frame is drawn 1 mm outside it, and a dashed line shows the stamp itself.
    2 Frames and captions
    Choose Thin, Medium or Double under Frame. Under Caption, the Heading goes above the stamp and its Name below. Value, condition, perforation and catalogue number are under More details: fill them in only when they help. An amber ! means a caption or frame runs into something or past the page border.
    3 Theme, page border and paper
    Click an empty part of the page to see the album's settings on the right. Theme colours the page border and headings, and Page border has plain borders for exhibition pages and decorative ones for personal albums. Paper changes the paper size and orientation.
    4 Preview and export
    Preview shows the page as it will print. Export makes a PDF, PNG or SVG that matches it, and File › Save keeps the album to work on later. Help › Show the tutorial brings these steps back.
