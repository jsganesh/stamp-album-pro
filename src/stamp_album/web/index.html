👋 Welcome to StampAlbum Pro

1 Add stamps at their catalogue size
Click a shape in the palette, or drag it onto the page. Type the stamp's size from the catalogue into W and H: the frame is drawn 1 mm outside it, and a dashed line shows the stamp itself.
2 Frames and captions
Choose Thin, Medium or Double under Frame. Type the description below the stamp; the heading, catalogue number and details go under Philatelic data. An amber ! means a caption or frame runs into something or past the page border.
3 Theme, page border and paper
Above the page, Theme colours the page border and headings, and Page border has plain borders for exhibition pages and decorative ones for personal albums. The Page button changes the paper size and orientation.
4 Preview and export
Preview shows the page as it will print. Export makes a PDF, PNG or SVG that matches it, and File › Save keeps the album to work on later.
